03 / A HISTORY OF EXCELLENCE

Since 1839.

Nearly two centuries of independent watchmaking in Geneva.

01 1839

A vision begins

The company is founded in Geneva by Antoine Norbert de Patek and François Czapek.

02 1845

A new mechanism

Jean Adrien Philippe develops his keyless winding and hand-setting system.

03 1932

A family legacy

The Stern family takes stewardship of the manufacture.

04 1976

An icon is born

The Nautilus introduces a new expression of elegant sporting watches.

05 1997

A contemporary spirit

The Aquanaut joins the collection with a distinctive modern character.
